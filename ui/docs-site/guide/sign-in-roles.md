# Sign in & user roles

Open the app and click **Sign in**. Enter your email, then your password.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Super Admin** | Manages tenants and platform settings across all organizations. |
| **Tenant Admin** | Full control inside one organization: users, enrollment, devices, schedules, reports. |
| **Supervisor** | Day-to-day monitoring: attendance, daily status, and reports. |

After signing in you land on the page that matches your role — tenant admins
and supervisors go to **Attendance**, super admins to the **Dashboard**.

::: tip Forgot your password?
Ask your tenant admin (or, for tenant admins, your platform administrator) to
reset it — self-service reset is not available yet.
:::
